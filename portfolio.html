Janna Feiguinova
Stylist · Production Manager

Janna Feiguinova is a seasoned stylist with over 15 years of experience in the fashion industry. Her journey began in marketing, where she honed her skills in understanding consumer trends and aesthetics. Eventually, her passion for style led her to the world of jewelry, where she developed a keen eye for detail and craftsmanship.

Educated in three renowned European styling schools, Janna blends her global influences with a deep understanding of personal expression. Over her career, she has styled more than 400 looks, working with everyone from CEOs and entrepreneurs to fashion icons and everyday individuals.

Janna's expertise extends beyond personal styling — she has worked as a production manager backstage at fashion shows, overseeing models, makeup artists, and designers. She's also styled her own runway shows at Brooklyn Fashion Week in 2023 and 2024, bringing her unique vision to life.

Case 01
Grazia Slovenia

Digital cover · "Zapisano v usodo" · Olga Kostina × Goizane Jayo

Cover · Grazia Slovenia

About the project

A digital cover story for Grazia Slovenia, shot on the streets of New York — from the Oculus in the Financial District to Fifth Avenue. The styling plays on the contrast between the city's architecture and near-theatrical silhouettes: a corset printed with classical painting, a dress built from white roses, a black lace look against a yellow cab, and burgundy feathers over liquid satin.

Jemma · NYC

About the project

Case 04
Bespoke Atelier

Editorial styling · bespoke high-end tailoring × fine jewelry brand

Bespoke Atelier · New York

About the project

Styled simultaneously for a bespoke atelier and a fine jewelry house, both high-end — three tailored looks across New York's hotel lobbies and landmark streets: an ivory double-breasted suit by the fireplace, an olive herringbone jacket at the Rockefeller clock, and a pinstripe power suit at dusk. The fittings themselves became part of the story — Janna adjusting each earring and clasp between frames.

Vintage Affair

About the project

A four-way collaboration built around a fleet of vintage and exotic convertibles, styled for a fine jewelry house and a high-end bridal atelier at once. Case 06
Artist & Muse

Lifestyle editorial · styled around the model's own paintings

Artist & Muse

About the project

The model behind this editorial is also a painter, so her own canvases became part of the set — an abstract bird study, a magenta gesture painting on the piano, a third resting against gilded frames. Eight looks move from animal print and a French beret to a bar-worthy white tuxedo dress, each staged alongside her work rather than in front of a plain backdrop.
